# Edge Cases and Failures

An index of the failures the chapters describe in their "Where it goes wrong" sections, and of
every entry under "What did not work" and "What we got wrong" in the [field notes](../notes.md).
One row per failure, merged across chapters and grouped by area. The rows are summaries: follow the
chapter links for the mechanism and the commands. To start from a failing policy rather than a
symptom, use [94 A Debugging Flowchart](94-debugging-flowchart.md).

How to read the rows:

- "Nothing" in the first column means exactly that: no error is raised and the training loss looks
  normal. These are the expensive ones.
- A number in a row is measured here and comes from the [lab notebook](../lab-notebook.md), unless
  the row says "arithmetic" or "background".
- "Not measured" means the chapters describe the mechanism from the code and no run here shows its
  effect on success.
- "Notes:" in the last column names the field-notes table the row answers.

## 1. Tokens, prompts and padding

| What you see | Why | How to check, what to do | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nothing. A short instruction is read one position later in a training batch than when served alone | The action token used to be appended after the batch padding. Both wrappers now insert it after the last real token (`insert_after_last_real_token` in `vision_language_backbones/base.py`). Effect on success not measured | Compare the conditioning of one prompt alone and inside a batch with a longer one; `tests/essential/models/test_backbone_conditioning.py` pins the layout | [10](10-tokens.md), [50](50-what-the-action-head-sees.md), [60](60-anatomy-of-a-vla.md), [61](61-pretrained-backbones.md), [93](93-train-inference-skew.md) |
| Nothing. With `conditioning: all_tokens` the action head sees another sequence length in a padded batch than when one prompt is served; or a `ValueError` naming `conditioning_len` | Without a fixed `conditioning_len` the conditioning keeps the batch's padded length; with it, a longer prompt raises in `conditioning_from_hidden_states` | Pass one sample alone and inside a padded batch and compare; set `conditioning_len` from the longest prompt (528 in `vla_custom_tokens_reactive.yaml`) | [10](10-tokens.md), [12](12-positions.md), [60](60-anatomy-of-a-vla.md), [61](61-pretrained-backbones.md), [102](102-case-pretrained-backbones.md) |
| Nothing: the end of a long instruction is dropped | The token count depends on the number of images times `img_num_tokens`, the template's wrapper ids, the tokenizer and details such as a trailing full stop; `_embed_text` keeps the first `text_tokens` ids (32 here), so the closing tokens go first, then the end of the instruction | Print `len(ids)` for the longest instruction with the config's image count; template and placeholders take 7 slots with two images, 9 with four | [10](10-tokens.md), [12](12-positions.md) |
| Closed at the named object near 33% at every checkpoint | No instruction reaches the action head: `text_tokens` is 0 or `data.processor` is `"none"`; or, with `processor: simple_vlm`, `img_num_tokens` left at the preset's 64 fills the 32 slots with placeholders and cuts the instruction off silently | Read both keys in `experiments/pickup/<run>/config.yaml`; set `img_num_tokens: 1` and keep the longest prompt (13 ids, 15 with history) within `text_tokens` | [51](51-language-conditioning.md) |
| An index error on CPU or a device-side assertion on GPU at the embedding lookup | A pad id outside the embedding table: a tokenizer without a pad token gets an added `[PAD]` one past the old vocabulary. The SmolVLM2 pad id (2) does not hit it | `_embed_text` multiplies ids by the mask before the lookup; `test_vit_backbone_text.py` checks that left-padded, right-padded and unpadded inputs agree | [10](10-tokens.md) |
| The same index error; or nothing in stage 1 and weights that do not fit the stage-2 VLM | `model.vocab_size` differs from the tokenizer's size: the preset has 50,432, `custom_llm.yaml` overrides it to 49,280 | `len(tok)` must equal `model.vocab_size`; check before stage 1 | [10](10-tokens.md) |
| Nothing while prompts fit; an over-long prompt loses its first ids, placeholders included, and `VLM.forward` fails its placeholder assertion | `SimpleVLMProcessor.__call__` forwards only `return_tensors`, `padding` and `max_length` and always truncates, so the tokenizer's own sides apply, and it truncates on the left. Stage 1 keeps the last 257 ids of every longer document (recorded in the roadmap's ledger; effect not measured) | Print `tok.padding_side` and `tok.truncation_side`; keep prompts shorter than `max_length` | [10](10-tokens.md), [30](30-joining-vision-and-language.md), [31](31-training-a-vlm.md) |
| Predictions for a sequence change with the padding in front of it | A left-padded batch went through `Transformer.forward` without the cache, where the mask is dropped and real tokens attend to pads | Print `tokenizer.padding_side`; neither stage sets it, so both use the tokenizer's default, right | [11](11-the-transformer-block.md) |
| `is_causal=False` passed to `forward` changes nothing | The code computes `is_causal or self.is_causal` | Set `is_causal: false` in the config, as the action-head configs do | [11](11-the-transformer-block.md) |
| A shape error in the position embedding, or attention narrower than the stream | `head_dim = hidden_dim // n_heads` has no check: an odd head width breaks the rotary embedding, an even one drops the remainder | Choose `n_heads` that divides `hidden_dim` into an even head width | [11](11-the-transformer-block.md) |

## 3. Generating text

| What you see | Why | How to check, what to do | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| A word or phrase repeated until the token limit | The decoding rule or a tiny model; the test fixture shows the symptom and does not separate the two | Generate the same prompt with `temperature=0` and with the defaults. Remedies (background, not measured here): sampling, a lower `max_new_tokens`, an EOS the model emits | [14](14-generation.md) |
| Nothing: output after a prompt not formatted as in training | Next-token probabilities were fitted only after prefixes in the training format | Print the prompt next to a training text: it must be a prefix; `vlm_check.py` cuts its prompt out of a templated caption | [14](14-generation.md) |
| Generation starts from a padding position; batched or uncached generation changes with padding | Both `generate` methods read the logits at position -1, and `SimpleVLMProcessor.__call__` pads a single prompt to 2,048 ids unless `padding=False`; `inference_vlm.py`'s strip then removes nothing. With `use_cache=False` the mask is dropped | Print `inputs["input_ids"].shape` and decode the last five ids before `generate`; use `vlm_check.py` for the from-scratch VLM; left-pad and keep `use_cache=True`, or generate one prompt at a time | [11](11-the-transformer-block.md), [14](14-generation.md) |
| Generation never stops, or stops late | With `eos_token_id=None` the loop runs to `max_new_tokens`; a batch stops only when every row emits EOS at the same generation step | Do not rely on EOS: `vlm_check.py` keeps the text before the first period | [14](14-generation.md) |
| Scores of a check change between runs | The defaults draw at random | Use `temperature=0` or `top_k=1` | [14](14-generation.md) |

## 4. Training a language model

| What you see | Why | How to check, what to do | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Out of memory after raising the batch or the sequence length | The logits are `[batch, tokens, 49280]` in float32: 6.5 GB per tensor at batch 128 x 256 tokens, which did not fit on machine A | Multiply it out first; keep the forward pass at 32 and accumulate (`--hparams.global_batch_size 128 --hparams.per_gpu_batch_size 32`) | [11](11-the-transformer-block.md), [13](13-training-a-language-model.md) |
| The warm-up is far shorter or longer than intended | `warmup` below 1 is a fraction of total steps, anything else a count of optimiser steps: `--hparams.warmup 1` is one step | Read the `LR:` field of the first log lines | [13](13-training-a-language-model.md) |
| Stage 1 or 2 hangs and is not restarted; or the next stage starts from a half-trained model | The `llm` and `vlm` stages of `custom_path.sh` call `main.py` directly, and its `latest` helper returns a run's highest-numbered checkpoint | `grep -c "Model has seen the desired number of samples"` on each stage's log must print a count above 0 | [13](13-training-a-language-model.md), [62](62-the-from-scratch-path.md) |

## 6. Vision-language models and the from-scratch path

| What you see | Why | How to check, what to do | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ValueError` from `pixel_shuffle`; weights that do not load into the model | With a factor above 1 the token count must be a perfect square (a class token breaks it) and the grid side divisible by the factor; weights trained with one factor do not fit another | Change `data.img_num_tokens` with the factor | [30](30-joining-vision-and-language.md) |
| Image-position hidden states do not change with the instruction | Expected: images come before the text and the language model is causal, so conditioning on image positions alone leaves the instruction out | Change the text on a small model and compare the image-position states | [30](30-joining-vision-and-language.md) |

## 7. Actions, flow matching and chunks

| What you see | Why | How to check, what to do | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| A loss that jumps or turns NaN | A channel that never varies: its `std` is 0, the scale is clamped to 1e-8, and the first deviation becomes enormous | Check the smallest `std` in `stats.json`; raise `data.normalization.epsilon` or drop the channel | [40](40-action-spaces.md) |
| A joint stops short of an extreme pose the demonstrations reach | `FlowMatchingScheduler` clamps to `clamp_range`, ±3 standard deviations | Check `(max - mean) / std` per channel; widen `model.noise_scheduler.clamp_range` | [40](40-action-spaces.md) |
| Nothing, or a suspiciously low open-loop error for a policy without history | `lowdim_past_timesteps or default` read 0 as unset and fed the state one control step in the future in training. Fixed with an `is None` check | A batch's `proprioception` has `lowdim_past_timesteps + 1` rows; `tests/essential/data/test_proprioception_past_steps.py` | [40](40-action-spaces.md), [42](42-chunks-and-execution.md), [52](52-history-and-shortcuts.md), [93](93-train-inference-skew.md) |
| Nothing: a policy without history gets a state one control step old | The client sends `[previous, current]`; only slicing from the end in `Policy.infer` prevents it, and no test imports `policy_server` | Send two distinct states and read `proprioception`; write the test | [42](42-chunks-and-execution.md), [93](93-train-inference-skew.md) |
| Nothing: the previous command is repeated at every re-plan and every later action runs one control step late | The chunk is read from the wrong index: the first row of a 16-row output is the previous action | Print the first returned action next to `prev_action`; `open_loop_check.py` compares from the anchor on | [42](42-chunks-and-execution.md) |
| The gripper closes on empty table, read as a sampler or re-plan problem | In the runs here it was never a draw between modes or chunks heading for different objects: where the failing policies closed did not depend on the scene. Notes: "What we got wrong" (the point between the objects) | Read the slope before touching the sampler or `--exec_horizon`; near 0 check the inputs, near 1 with centimetre misses sweep the flow steps; call `Policy.infer` twice without a `noise_seed` to see two draws | [41](41-flow-matching.md), [42](42-chunks-and-execution.md), [92](92-measuring-failure.md) |
| Fewer flow steps cost points on some policies | Two flow steps equal ten on five of seven policies and cost 12 and 19 points on the other two (77% against 89% and 96%); against the notebook's Standard column the policy with random starts, recoveries and early windows repeated also loses 12 (74/100 against 86/100) | Sweep `--flow_steps` again on every new policy; use a divisor of 1000 (with 7 the loop covers 1.136 of the path, arithmetic) | [41](41-flow-matching.md), [72](72-which-demonstrations-teach.md) |
| Planning again more often does not help | 2 actions executed per chunk gave 76%, 8 gave 85%, the T block 8/23 against 15/23. Notes: "What did not work" | Keep `--exec_horizon 8`; re-planning is not looking | [42](42-chunks-and-execution.md) |
| The same starting noise for every chunk of an episode makes it worse | 39%, 55% and 80% at 2, 4 and 8 actions per chunk (`--noise episode`). Notes: "What did not work" | Keep `--noise fresh` | [42](42-chunks-and-execution.md) |
| Sampling fails or trains against the wrong target with another scheduler; a ported sampler walks away from the data | Only `FlowMatchingScheduler.step` accepts `step_size=`; here $s = 0$ is the action and the target is noise minus action, while the papers put noise at time 0 (background) | Keep `use_flow_matching_scheduler: true`; flip both sign and time when porting | [41](41-flow-matching.md) |
